Free Stories

Free stories can be used without creating an account or signing in. If free stories do not load, check your internet connection and try again.

Premium Access and Subscriptions

Premium features may require Sign in with Apple and an active subscription. If you already subscribed, use “Restore Purchases” in the app to restore access.

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. You can review or cancel subscriptions in your iPhone settings under your Apple ID subscription settings.

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is used only when needed for premium features, subscriptions, or restoring purchases. Free stories remain available without signing in.
