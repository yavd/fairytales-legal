Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Wishling handles information when you use the app.

Home Support

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Overview

Wishling is a children’s bedtime story app available on iOS and Android. It is designed to provide a simple and safe reading experience. You can browse and read free stories without creating an account.

You only need to sign in or create an account when you choose to use premium features, purchase or restore a subscription, or sync your account across devices.

Information We May Collect

Free use without an account

If you use only free stories, we do not require you to create an account, enter an email address, username, or password.

Account and sign-in

If you create an account or sign in, we may collect the details needed to identify your account:

  • Email and password: if you register with an email address, we store your email and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Sign in with Apple: Apple may provide your name and email address depending on your Apple ID settings. If you choose “Hide My Email,” Apple provides a private relay address instead of your personal email.
  • Sign in with Google: Google may provide your name, email address, and a Google account identifier so we can create or access your account.

Subscription information

If you purchase or restore a subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive the subscription status and purchase identifiers needed to unlock premium content, verify your subscription, and manage access inside the app.

Usage and app activity

To keep the app working and improve it, we may collect limited usage information such as app version, non-precise events (for example, opening or completing a story), story ratings (likes/dislikes) and feedback you submit, and responses to in-app polls.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device push token (via Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send updates such as new-story announcements. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Support messages

If you contact us for support, we may receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

How We Use Information

  • To provide access to free and premium stories.
  • To create and secure your account and support sign-in.
  • To verify subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • To send optional notifications you have allowed.
  • To respond to support or privacy requests.
  • To maintain app functionality, security, and reliability.

Children’s Privacy

Wishling is intended for families and children’s story reading. We do not require children to create accounts to read free stories, and we do not show behavioral advertising or use personal information to profile children. Parents or guardians should manage account sign-in, subscriptions, and support communication.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers necessary to operate the app, including:

  • Apple and Google for sign-in, in-app purchases, and subscription management.
  • Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores app content, account data, and backend functionality on our behalf.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the app, support subscriptions, respond to support requests, comply with legal obligations, or maintain security. When you delete your account, we remove your personal account data as described below.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can use free stories without creating an account.
  • You can choose whether to sign in with email, Apple, or Google for premium features.
  • You can delete your account and associated personal data directly in the app (in your account settings), or by contacting us.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID or Google Play settings.
  • You can turn notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • You can contact us to ask privacy-related questions or request access to or deletion of your data.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at hello.fairytalesapp@gmail.com.
