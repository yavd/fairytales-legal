Privacy Policy

FairyTales • Effective May 27, 2026

FairyTales is a personalized story reading app. This page explains how the app handles information when you read, listen, download, and personalize stories.

📖 Overview

FairyTales lets users read, listen to, download, and personalize fairy tales and stories. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use FairyTales.

1. Information We May Collect

FairyTales may collect account information such as username, email address, sign-in information, subscription status, app usage data, story interaction data, and technical information needed to operate and improve the app.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide app features, manage user accounts, enable premium access, restore purchases, improve app performance, respond to support requests, and maintain app security.

3. Personalized Stories

If you choose to replace names or personalize story content, that information is used to provide the requested feature. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into story fields.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

Premium access and subscriptions are handled through Apple. FairyTales does not directly collect or store your full payment card information. Apple may process purchase and subscription information according to Apple’s own policies.

5. Third-Party Services

FairyTales may use backend hosting, database, analytics, authentication, or other technical services to operate the app. These services may process limited technical data needed to provide the app experience.

6. Children’s Privacy

FairyTales is designed to provide a safe reading experience. Parents or guardians should supervise children’s use of the app. We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to request support, ask privacy questions, or request deletion of information associated with your account where applicable.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or account requests, contact us at hello.fairytalesapp@gmail.com.
