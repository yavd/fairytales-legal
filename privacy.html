Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fairytales handles information when you use the app.

Home Support

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Overview

Fairytales is designed to provide a simple and safe reading experience. You can browse and read free stories without creating an account.

Sign in with Apple is only required when you choose to use premium features, purchase or restore a subscription, or connect your account for subscription access.

Information We May Collect

Free use without an account

If you use only free stories, we do not require you to create an account, enter an email address, username, or password.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple may provide your name and email address, depending on your Apple ID settings and your choices. If you choose “Hide My Email,” Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your personal email address.

Subscription information

If you purchase or restore a subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple. We may receive subscription status information needed to unlock premium content and manage access inside the app.

Support messages

If you contact us for support, we may receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

How We Use Information

  • To provide access to free and premium stories.
  • To support Sign in with Apple and subscription restoration.
  • To respond to support or privacy requests.
  • To maintain app functionality, security, and reliability.

Children’s Privacy

Fairytales is intended for families and children’s story reading. We do not require children to create accounts to read free stories. Parents or guardians should manage subscriptions, Apple ID sign-in, and support communication.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers necessary to operate the app, such as Apple services for Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases, and hosting services for app content and backend functionality.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the app, support subscriptions, respond to support requests, comply with legal obligations, or maintain security.

Your Choices

  • You can use free stories without creating an account.
  • You can choose whether to sign in with Apple for premium features.
  • You can manage subscriptions through your Apple ID settings.
  • You can contact us to ask privacy-related questions.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at hello.fairytalesapp@gmail.com.
